# Campus Market Terms and Community Rules

Version 2026-10 (the version the app records when you accept). **Draft for team review before the public beta; not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Items marked *Decision* need a team decision before release.

## 1. What Campus Market is

Campus Market helps students and their neighbors buy, sell, give away and ask for secondhand items. Each participating school has a campus market that only its verified students can see, and an open market is available to anyone who signs up with an email address.

Campus Market is a place for buyers and sellers to find each other. **We are not a party to any trade.** We do not handle, hold or transfer payments, and we do not inspect, store or ship items. Buyers and sellers meet and pay each other at their own risk, and we are not responsible for the condition of items or for payments made between users.

## 2. Your account

- You must be at least 18 years old to use Campus Market. *Decision: confirm 18+, which other meetup marketplaces require, or allow 13 to 17 with a parent's permission.*
- You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. One email can hold one account. Addresses with `+` and temporary email services cannot be used.
- A school email from a participating school places you in that school's campus market. If your school email stops working after you leave school, you lose access to the campus market and can use the open market with another email.
- Your nickname is public; your email address and real name are never shown to other users. Do not choose a nickname that impersonates someone or that our rules prohibit.
- You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell us at the contact address below if you think someone else has accessed it.

## 3. Listings and trades

- List only items you own and have the right to sell, described honestly with photos of the actual item.
- A trade is agreed in chat with a price, time and place. Meet at a public place, such as the meetup spots suggested in each trade, and inspect the item before you pay.
- Confirm a trade as completed only after the exchange happens. Unconfirmed trades complete automatically 72 hours after the meetup time unless someone reports a problem.
- Cancel as early as you can if plans change. Cancelling an accepted trade close to the meetup time adds a penalty point.

## 4. Prohibited items

These cannot be listed in any market:

- Alcohol; tobacco, vapes and nicotine products; cannabis and drugs; drug paraphernalia
- Prescription medicine and prescription medical devices
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives and fireworks
- Stolen, counterfeit or recalled goods
- Academic dishonesty materials, such as exam answers, completed assignments or answer keys
- Gift cards, accounts, digital codes and personal data
- Live animals
- Sexually explicit images, adult services and adult products, including 18+ rated games and media
- Event tickets, housing and subleases
- Hazardous materials

We may remove a listing that breaks these rules without notice.

## 5. Community rules

1. **Keep your word.** Show up for meetups you accept, or cancel early.
2. **Be honest.** Describe items accurately, including flaws.
3. **Be respectful.** No harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination or sexual content in chat, listings or profiles.
4. **Stay safe.** Meet in public places, ideally in daylight. Do not share your home address or financial details in chat.
5. **Keep trades on Campus Market until you meet.** Do not use the app to advertise other services or send spam.
6. **Report problems.** Use Report and Block on any listing, message or user that breaks these rules.

## 6. Reviews, trust grades and penalties

- After a completed trade, both sides can leave a review with a rating and tags.
- Each user has a trust grade from C to A+, starting at B. Completed trades and good reviews raise it. Poor reviews or one violation can lower it to B−, and repeated violations to C.
- Penalty points come from upheld no-shows, harassment, late cancellations and claims judged to be made in bad faith. Each penalty expires 90 days after it is given and is listed, with its expiry date, on your account status page.
- How grades are calculated is not published, so that they stay hard to game. Points can never buy a grade.

## 7. Reports and disputes

- Either side of an accepted meetup trade can report a no-show from 15 minutes to 48 hours after the meetup time. The other side has 48 hours to respond.
- Evidence includes photos taken in the app, an optional location check-in taken only when you tap "I'm here", and written statements. The reviewer can also see the trade's chat. Evidence is used only for that dispute.
- The reviewer decides whether a claim is more likely true than not. An upheld claim gives the other side penalty points; a claim judged knowingly false gives the person who made it penalty points.
- Dispute decisions affect trust grades and account status only. They never move money.

## 8. Points and cosmetics

- You earn points for completed trades, reviews, giveaways and daily check-ins, within daily limits.
- Points have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money or goods outside the app.
- Purchases of cosmetics are final. Items you own stay yours if they are later removed from the shop.
- We may correct balances that result from errors or abuse, including below zero, and may change point values, prices and items with notice in the app. If points are discontinued, no compensation is owed.

## 9. Enforcement

Depending on how serious and how frequent a violation is, we may give a warning, remove content, suspend an account for 1 to 30 days, or ban it permanently. Every action records its reason, and you can see actions on your account on the account status page. A banned email can never be used to sign up again, and deleting an account does not clear its record.

We may inform a student's school of verified violations of these Terms, such as fraud, threats or harassment.

The license ends when the content is deleted, except for copies we must keep for disputes, safety or legal reasons.

## 11. Privacy

- During sign-up you choose whether to share usage data to improve the app and whether to allow personalized deals. You can change both choices anytime in Settings.
- Data shared outside Campus Market is only ever de-identified aggregates; we commit not to try to re-identify it.
- Photos of sold or closed listings, chats both sides have left, and dispute evidence are deleted on fixed schedules.
- You can delete your account in the app. Your personal data is then removed; trades, reviews and messages you took part in remain and show "Deleted user".
- We may disclose account information in response to a subpoena, court order, search warrant or other valid legal process, and when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent physical harm.

A separate privacy policy with the full details is required before the public beta.
